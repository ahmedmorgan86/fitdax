# FitDax movement illustrations

Created for this project on 22 October 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool, not an application API. FitDax does not call an image or language model at runtime.

These are generated educational illustrations, not source photographs or clinically validated movement demonstrations. The Free Exercise DB publisher's Unlicense applies to its original photographs, not to these generated files. Do not remove the generated-illustration label from the app. Independent trainer review remains necessary before treating any illustration as authoritative instruction.

Shared design brief: photorealistic two-panel movement sequence on a plain white studio background; the same adult male athlete in a charcoal shirt and black shorts; full body visible; consistent camera, lighting and equipment; anatomically plausible positions; no logos or embedded text.

Movement-specific prompt briefs:

- `kettlebell-halo.png`: front chest hold, then bell passing close behind the head, elbows bent; stable torso.
- `kettlebell-halo-extension.png`: kettlebell behind head with bent elbows, then elbows extending overhead, without back arching.
- `kettlebell-triceps-extension.png`: two-handed overhead kettlebell triceps extension, extended and lowered phases.
- `chair-squat.png`: stand in front of a stable chair, then sit back until lightly touching the seat, feet supported.
- `wall-pushup.png`: hands against a wall with straight arms, then bend elbows and lean toward the wall while keeping the body aligned.
- `bird-dog.png`: stable hands-and-knees position, then opposite arm and leg extended, level trunk.
- `marching.png`: alternating right and left raised knees with opposite arms. An initial duplicated-leg result was edited to show opposite legs.

The briefs above summarize the image-generation instructions. Original generated files were copied into this directory; the final marching edit replaced the initial result.
